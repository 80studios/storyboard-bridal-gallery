Dramatic Ballgown Bridal | Old Money Library Editorial

Some bridal portraits are styled. This one is observed. A voluminous satin ballgown with a cathedral train pooled across a dark wood library floor, the skirt spreading wide enough to fill the room. The tufted slate blue velvet chair behind her is the only thing that holds still. Sculptural old money bridal at its most considered.
